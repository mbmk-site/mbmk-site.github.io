The Fall of the al-Assad Dynasty and the Uncertain Future of Syria

What happened in Syria on 11 December 2024?

The nation of Syria got a wind of freedom on 11 December 2024, when the extremist(deemed by west) group HTS(Hay’at Tahrir al-Sham) and other rebel forces captured the capital of Syria, Damascus and ousted the hereditary dictatorship which controlled Syria since 1971, and this 11 of the December also marked the end of the Syrian Civil War which started in 2011. But the dictatorship led by Bashar al-Assad( from 2000 onwards, before that it was his father who ruled Syria; Hafez al-Assad) did not get punished as Bashar and the al-Assad family escaped and seeked asylum from Russia before they got captured. After this the people of Syria celebrated the freedom in joy, but nevertheless the stability of the state of Syria is still in uncertainty.

Who are the al-Assad?

The al-Assad is a political family from Syria who ruled the nation from 1971, when military officer Hafez al-Assad(1930-2000) became the president after the 1970 coup in Syria and his family continued to rule the nation till 11 December 2024. When Hafez got Syria in his hands, he affiliated and made all the high ranking members of the Ba’ath Party(a political party which ruled Syria from 1963) loyal to him and his family. Then Hafez ruled Syria until 2000, when he died in a ripe old age, and one of his sons, Bassel was chose to be the next president but he died in an accident the year, so his other son Bashar, who was studying ophthalmology in Western Eye Hospital in London was immediately called to Syria then on 17th July 2000 he was hailed as the president of Syria till he was ousted in 2024.

The Uncertain Future of Syria

Syria after the revolution is joyfully celebrating the freedom that they got, but the future is uncertain, as nations which have been revolutionized by organizations like HTS have experienced severe problems, eg: Libya and Afghanistan. As we do not know much about HTS’s real intention, but thing can also get better, but we cannot say anything now. Syria has also got an interim government under prime minister Mohammed al-Bashir.
